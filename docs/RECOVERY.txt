PECKER TAP R36S RECOVERY

Enter recovery mode from a Mac:
1. Power the R36S off completely.
2. Remove the OS microSD card and insert it into the Mac.
3. Open the FAT partition named BOOT.
4. Create an empty file at the top level named exactly:
   PECKERTAP_RECOVERY
5. Eject the card safely, return it to TF1, and power on.

In recovery mode the game does not start. TTY1 and SSH are enabled for
maintenance. Remove PECKERTAP_RECOVERY from BOOT and reboot to resume the game.

Useful commands in recovery:
  systemctl status peckertap
  journalctl -u peckertap -b
  systemctl restart peckertap
  cat /proc/device-tree/model
  cat /sys/class/drm/card0-DSI-1/modes 2>/dev/null
  evtest
  aplay -l

If the display is black after a fresh image:
- Power off; do not repeatedly hard-reset while filesystems are writing.
- Restore the three current-card files saved under
  BOOT/PeckerTap-Hardware-Backup one at a time only as directed.
- The build backup is also at:
  ~/Desktop/peckertap-r36s/hardware-backup/BOOT

The current card appears to have Panel 5 history, but final panel compatibility
must be established by physical boot testing.
